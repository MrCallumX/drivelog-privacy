Drive Log – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Drive Log is made by Tappet Labs, an independent developer in the United Kingdom ("we"). This policy explains what the Drive Log Android app does with your information.

The short version

Everything you enter stays on your phone. We do not collect, receive or share any of your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere by itself.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores the records you create, on your phone only, in the app's private storage:

  • trips (start and finish time, optional number plate, optional note, optional start odometer);
  • fuel entries (time, number plate, litres, cost, odometer);
  • a few app settings and the state of a running trip (for example whether you have seen the welcome screen).

Other apps cannot read this storage, and Android's cloud backup of the app is switched off.

Location

While a trip is running, the app uses your phone's GPS to tell whether the car has stopped moving, so it can remind you to stop the timer after about 10 minutes stationary. Location is used only while a trip is running and you can see the ongoing "Driving" notification. Location readings are kept in memory only for that check. They are never written to storage, never included in trips, exports or backups, and never leave your phone. The app does not use location in the background when no trip is running.

Backups and exports

You can choose to export a CSV file or save a backup file. This happens only when you start it. You choose where the file goes (for example your phone's storage, a cloud drive or an email app you pick). Once a file is saved or shared outside the app, it is handled by the place you sent it to, under that service's own privacy terms. We never receive a copy.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual trips and fuel entries in the app. To delete everything, uninstall the app or use Android Settings > Apps > Drive Log > Storage > Clear storage. Any backup or export files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete there.

Support link

If the app shows a Ko-fi support link, tapping it opens Ko-fi in your phone's browser. Drive Log itself sends nothing to Ko-fi. Anything you do on Ko-fi is covered by Ko-fi's own privacy terms.

Children

Drive Log is meant for adult drivers. It is not aimed at children, and it does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

Because we do not collect or hold any personal data about you, we have no personal data to give you, correct or erase. Your records are under your control on your device. If you have a question or concern, contact us below. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy and the date at the top before that version is released.
